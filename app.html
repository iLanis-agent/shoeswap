ShoeSwap

Convert between US, UK and EU adult shoe sizes using the real maths behind them: barleycorns and Paris points.
US and UK sizes count barleycorns (1/3 inch); EU counts Paris points (2/3 cm) on the last, which runs about 2 cm longer than the foot. Formulas: US men = 3 x foot inches - 22, US women = 3 x foot inches - 21, UK = 3 x foot inches - 23 (Wikipedia, Shoe size). Brands differ by up to a full size, so use this as a guide and check the brand chart. Adult sizes only. Mondopoint is the foot length in mm rounded up to 5.
